# Screen Time Guard Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 October 2026

Screen Time Guard is a parental-control utility designed for parents and guardians who want to set viewing and break periods for supported video apps on an Android device.

## Data handling

Screen Time Guard is designed to operate locally on the device. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account sign-in, cloud storage, or a remote server.

The app uses Android Usage Access to determine whether supported apps such as YouTube or YouTube Kids are currently in the foreground. This information is used only on the device to operate the viewing timer and break countdown.

The app does not upload, sell, share, or transmit usage information, PINs, timer settings, or other personal information to the developer or third parties.

## Permissions and special access

- **Usage Access:** Used to detect when a supported video app is in the foreground.
- **Display over other apps:** Used to display the parent-configured break countdown over a supported video app while a break is active.
- **Notifications / foreground service:** Used to keep the screen-time monitor user-visible while it is running.
- **Modify audio settings:** Used to mute media from a supported video app while the break screen is active and restore the prior media volume afterwards.
- **Run at startup:** Used to resume monitoring after device restart when monitoring was enabled by the parent.

## Parental control and monitoring

Screen Time Guard is intended for parental control. Monitoring is visible through an ongoing Android notification while enabled. A parent can stop monitoring from the app.

## Children

The app is intended to be configured and controlled by an adult parent or guardian. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Security

The parent PIN is stored locally as a salted cryptographic hash. The app does not transmit the PIN off the device.

## Data deletion

Because app settings are stored locally, users can delete them by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Third-party services

The app detects installed use of YouTube and YouTube Kids only for its parental timer function. Screen Time Guard is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact address published with the app's Google Play listing.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
