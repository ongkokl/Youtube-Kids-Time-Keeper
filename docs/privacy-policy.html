Screen Time Guard Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Screen Time Guard is a parental-control utility designed for parents and guardians who want to set viewing and break periods for supported video apps on an Android device.

Data handling

Screen Time Guard operates locally on the device. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account sign-in, cloud storage, or a remote server. Android Usage Access is used only to determine whether supported apps such as YouTube or YouTube Kids are currently in the foreground. Usage information, PINs and timer settings are not uploaded, sold, shared or transmitted.

Permissions and special access

Usage Access detects a supported video app in the foreground. Display over other apps shows the break countdown over the supported app during a break. Notifications and a foreground service keep monitoring user-visible. Modify audio settings mutes supported video playback while the break screen is active and restores the prior volume. Run at startup resumes monitoring after restart when enabled by the parent.

Parental control and monitoring

Screen Time Guard is intended for parental control. Monitoring is visible through an ongoing Android notification while enabled, and a parent can stop monitoring from the app.

Children

The app is intended to be configured and controlled by an adult parent or guardian. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Security

The parent PIN is stored locally as a salted cryptographic hash and is not transmitted off the device.

Data deletion

Users can delete local app settings by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

Screen Time Guard detects use of YouTube and YouTube Kids only for its parental timer function. Screen Time Guard is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact address published with the app's Google Play listing.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated for material changes.
